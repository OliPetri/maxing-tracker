Selected factual benchmarks researched 2026-09-06. See [RESEARCH-NOTES.md](RESEARCH-NOTES.md) for the sources actually consulted, including Wiki mirrors and supplementary guides. This is a bundled, manually reviewed snapshot, not live scraping. Some indexed pages are several months old; Sailing is explicitly flagged as potentially outdated. Rates are estimates at the stated benchmark, not guarantees or automatic level scaling. Level is a benchmark in this skill; other requirements are described in notes and on the linked page. No unlock checks are performed. Lower endpoints are used where noted. Hitpoints, potion production, goats and leechfin cutting include explicitly derived estimates.

AFK/low-effort labels describe interaction, not a promise of uninterrupted idling. Ironman options do not automatically include supply gathering: see each method’s conditions.
